# Cosmic Realm — World Atlas

20 open maps in four sector rows. Difficulty is the zone's **enemy tier**
(higher tier = tougher spawns, denser danger, better rewards). Every map has
a preserved unique planet, a hand-tuned biome, a painted landmark structure,
an ambient energy effect, and themed decoration clusters that tell a local
story.

## Stations — 71 docks across the galaxy

Station kinds: **hub** (full services), **trade** (commodity exchange),
**military**, **mining**, **outpost**, **factory** (ore refinery + upgrades).
Docking opens the station interface: Bounties, Missions, Skills, Shipyard,
Loadout, Drones, Market, Services (factories swap in the Refinery tab).

Notable docks per region:

- **Earth row** — Helix Station (starter hub, 1-1), Iron Belt Refinery,
  Azure Trade Port & Veiled Outpost (1-2), Ember Citadel & Scarlet Shipyards
  (1-3), Echo Anchorage & Obsidian Free Port (1-4), Ironclad Bastion &
  Forge Gate Depot (1-5), plus Alpha Foundry and more.
- **Mars row** — Solar Haven & Corona Market (2-1), Rift Base Omega &
  Null-Point Station (2-2), Void Heart Station & Abyss Anchorage (2-3),
  Deep Field Haven & Iron Depth Exchange (2-4), Eye of the Storm &
  Wreckage Point (2-5), factories included.
- **Venus row** — Cloud Gate Station (hub, 3-1), Sulphur Port & Wind Market
  (3-2), Acid Citadel & Pressure Yards (3-3), Core Refuge & Cradle Station
  (3-4), Venusian Bastion, Eye Bazaar & Singularity Dock (3-5), plus cloud
  foundries and acid refineries.
- **Danger zones** — every 4-x map keeps at least a trade post and an
  outpost alive: Rift Outpost, Dead Zone Bazaar, Pirate Freeport & Pirate
  Stronghold, Null Station, Abyss Gate Station.

Station visuals: five distinct 3D models (three space-station hulls, two
factory hulls) assigned per zone so no map shows the same dock twice.

## Travel

Warp gates (portals) connect neighboring maps along each faction row and
bridge into the danger zones. The galaxy map (`m`) shows all sectors; gate
jumps count toward exploration missions and the Pathfinder milestone.

## Environment tech (short version)

Each map layers: color fill → star field (clusters, star clouds, milky
bands) → distant galaxies → multi-patch nebula → energy effect (subtle
alpha-breathing) → cosmic dust + glow regions → preserved planet → painted
landmark → decor sprites → foreground debris band → drifting asteroids →
ambient motes. Full detail: `RENDERING_PIPELINE.md`; art provenance:
`ASSET_LICENSES.md`.
