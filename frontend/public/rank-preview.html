RANK BADGE SIZING

Same nameplate markup as in-game. Left = new sizing, right = the previous height-only sizing. The source art runs from 37×64 (ratio 0.58) to 135×64 (ratio 2.11) — with height-only sizing every badge was 32px tall but the wide low ranks grew to 44px across, which is why they looked oversized next to the narrow high ranks.

New: equal painted area, not an equal box. The box is derived per badge from its ratio, so a wide insignia gets a bigger box and ends up showing the same amount of art as a near-square one. Two targets: 930px² for rank 0 and 7-22, 400px² for the entry ranks 1-6. This is what makes Chief General (1.45) match General (1.11).
